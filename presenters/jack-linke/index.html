Jack Linke builds (and breaks) things in Django and shares what he learns along the way through writing and speaking engagements.

For the past eight years he has been Managing Director and core programmer for Watervize, a Django-based platform used by irrigation districts and the farms and organizations they serve to streamline operations, improve analysis, and communicate more effectively.

His technical interests tend toward "real-world data" - GIS, graph/network modeling, simulation, and storytelling with visualizations.

Jack contributes to open source, maintains several somewhat-niche Python packages, and is a DSF Individual Member and PSF Contributing Member.

He has presented at both DjangoCon US and PyCon US, and his day job background includes years of technical briefing and instruction as a Marine Corps officer.

Outside work, he's usually adventuring with his husky, Lady Duchess, or photographing everything from fungi to cityscapes.
