Dan Ryan is a technologist consultant and web developer with two decades of industry experience, focused on human-centered design; digital access and justice; and accessibility. They currently serve as chair of the Django Software Foundation's Code of Conduct Working Group, where they have led the effort to modernize Django's community standards by adopting Contributor Covenant 3.0. Beyond their work in the Django community, Dan has advised companies and organizations on technology and security, served as Director of Front-end Development for President Obama's 2012 presidential campaign, and contributes to digital equity work in the Chattanooga, Tennessee region.
