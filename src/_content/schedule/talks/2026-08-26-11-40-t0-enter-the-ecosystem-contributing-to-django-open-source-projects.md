Taking that first step into open source can feel overwhelming, but it doesn't have to be. This talk is a friendly, practical guide for developers who are curious about contributing to projects in the Django ecosystem but haven't made that first contribution yet.

We'll start by asking yourself why you want to contribute in the first place. Having a clear goal will keep you motivated when you hit those inevitable roadblocks. From there, we'll walk through how to find a project that aligns with your interests, skills, and availability, how to take the time to understand a project's contribution process, and how to find the right first issue, whether that's a code change, a documentation update, a bug report, or a code review.

We'll also cover what happens after you submit your work, how to receive feedback with an open mind, iterate, and celebrate getting that first pull request or change merged. We'll also touch on how to use AI tools responsibly as part of your workflow.

You'll leave with a clear, actionable path to your first contribution and a curated list of resources to help you keep the momentum going.
