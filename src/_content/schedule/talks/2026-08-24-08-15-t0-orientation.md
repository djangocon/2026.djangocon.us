Are you:
-  attending your first tech conference?
-  attending your first DjangoCon US?
-  attending DjangoCon US alone?
-  a member of an underrepresented group?

If you answered "yes" to any or all of those, Orientation is for you!

DjangoCon US is packed with awesome people and awesome information. But that much awesome can be overwhelming.  Orientation is designed to help you get your bearings on all the awesome. As a bonus, it will also help you find your way around the venue!

Living your best life is up to you, but we *can* help you have your best DjangoCon US.

We look forward to helping you start your DjangoCon US journey in the best way possible!
