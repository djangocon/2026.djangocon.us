Kick off the sprints by hearing from project leads as they introduce the projects looking for contributors. A great way to find a project that interests you before you settle in for the day.

### If you're leading a project

Keep it to about 30 seconds per project. A good blurb covers:

- **What the project does:** a one-sentence description for people who've never heard of it.
- **What you're working on at sprints:** the specific goal(s) for you at sprints
- **Experience required:** skills, languages, or experience contributors should already have, or "none, we'll pair with you."
- **Where to find you:** where in the room you'll be sitting, or how to spot you.
