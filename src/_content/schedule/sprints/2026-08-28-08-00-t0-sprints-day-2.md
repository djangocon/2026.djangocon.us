DjangoCon US sprints are where the wider Django community comes together to build. Pull up a chair, find a project that interests you, and start [contributing to Django](https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/dev/internals/contributing/sprint-quickstart/), [django-language-server](https://djls.joshthomas.dev), or another community-driven open source project.

Please see the `#sprints` channel on the conference Slack for help getting started!

Everyone is welcome (including virtual attendees!), whatever your experience level. Whether it's your first ever contribution or your hundredth, there's a place for you and plenty of people happy to help you get started.
