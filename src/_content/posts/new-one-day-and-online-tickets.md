Can't make it to Chicago for the whole week? We've added new **One-Day** tickets to make it easier to join us for just the part you can get to.

<!-- excerpt -->

## What's New

One-Day tickets are available in both **Individual** and **Corporate** rates, the same way our regular tickets work:

*   **One-Day Individual (In-person)**: $199
*   **One-Day Corporate (In-person)**: $349

Pick the day that fits your schedule, and you're set.

And **every One-Day ticket includes online access for the full week.** So you're not just buying a day pass to come see us in Chicago, you're also getting to watch along with us online the rest of the week.

We know schedules are busy. People have work. School is starting back up for a lot of folks. This gives you an option that fits around all of that. Plus you can stream and watch at your leisure.

## Online Tickets

We also have **Online** tickets. If travel isn't in the cards this year, you can still catch the talks and be part of the conference from wherever you are:

*   **Online Individual**: $99
*   **Online Corporate**: $199

## Get Your Ticket

All ticket types, including One-Day, Corporate, Individual, and Online, are on our Tito page:

[ti.to/defna/djangocon-us-2026](https://ti.to/defna/djangocon-us-2026)

DjangoCon US 2026 runs August 24th through 28th at the coco Chicago Downtown. Whether it's for a day, the whole week, or from your couch, we'll see you soon!
