DjangoCon US 2026 is almost here! Start your conference week with us at the [Welcome Reception](/talks/welcome-reception/) on Sunday, August 23, from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM in the 15th Floor Lobby.

<!-- excerpt -->

Whether this is your first DjangoCon US or one of many, the Welcome Reception is a relaxed chance to meet fellow attendees before the talks begin. Stop by for conversation and beverages, make some new friends, and say hello to old ones.

## Welcome Reception details

- **When:** Sunday, August 23, 2026, from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM Central Time ([view in your time zone](https://time.is/compare/0700PM_23_August_2026_in_Chicago))
- **Where:** 15th Floor Lobby
