After a career as an accountant and a university instructor, Kojo became a software developer. He's been active in the Python & Django communities since 2013, a [DjangoCon US](https://djangocon.us/) organizer since 2016 & the [DEFNA](https://www.defna.org/) North American Ambassador since 2017. He's also led the [Newcomer Orientation](https://us.pycon.org/2023/events/newcomer-orientation/) for PyCon US since 2022.
He's currently a software engineer at [REVSYS](https://twitter.com/revsys). He has been fortunate enough to have spoken at multiple conferences in multiple countries on most continents.

While he's much less active on social media than he used to be, you can find Kojo on Mastodon as [@kojoidrissa@fosstodon.org](https://fosstodon.org/@kojoidrissa). And sometimes on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/kojoidrissa.bsky.social).
