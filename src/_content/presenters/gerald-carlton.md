Gerald majored in Computer Science at A&T State University. While there, he gained an appreciation for developing quality software beyond simply writing and shipping code. He loves the nuances of testing software, seeing QA analysts as explorers of digital territory in the name of high functionality. His collaborative and exploratory approach ensures the usability and quality of our tools across browsers, devices, and environments.

In his time off, Gerald likes to cook comfort food dishes at home and rumor has it he makes a fierce lasagna. He also enjoys playing video games and binging on the latest television show with his wife.
