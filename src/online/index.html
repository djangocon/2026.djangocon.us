Watch the Livestream

This year, all talks run on a single stage, so you won't have to choose between overlapping sessions. Click through to the stage from the Schedule to access the live stream, and chat with speakers and other attendees in Slack while you watch.

Check out the Stage

Can't catch a talk live? No worries — recordings will be made available to you after the conference.

Where to Chat

Join us on Slack and say hello! A few channels to get you started:

  • #hallway-general-chat — announcements and conference-wide chat
  • #online-conference — chat with other online attendees
  • #sprints — team up with other folks to work on Django!

Need Help?

Having technical issues with video, audio, or login? Email us at hello@djangocon.us for support, or reach out in Slack and someone will jump in to assist.
