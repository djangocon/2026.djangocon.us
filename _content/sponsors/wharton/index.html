For more than 25 years, Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS) has supported 500+ institutions across 38 countries with trusted data, research tools, and analytics. Partnering with global data vendors, WRDS hosts over 400 TB of data across disciplines including Accounting, Banking, Economics, ESG, Finance, Healthcare, Insurance, Marketing, and Statistics. Users access data through a Django- and Wagtail-powered platform, Jupyter, PostgreSQL, SSH on the WRDS Cloud, and SAS. Serving more than 75,000 academic, commercial, and government users, WRDS is the global gold standard in data management, analytics, and research services, backed by the Wharton School.
